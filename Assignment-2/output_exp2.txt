OBSERVATIONS — FIRST PING
  • ICMP and ARP both appeared at 0.000 sec — ICMP triggered ARP immediately.
  • Switch flooded ARP broadcast to all ports — PC1 and PC2 both received it.
  • Only PC1 responded with ARP Reply since its IP address matched the request.
  • Switch dynamically learned MAC addresses of PC0 and PC1 from the traffic.
  • ICMP Echo Request was sent only after ARP resolution completed at 0.004 sec.
  • From sequence 2 onwards (1.012 sec), only ICMP was seen — ARP cache active.
  • Total events recorded: 21+ (ARP + ICMP combined).
